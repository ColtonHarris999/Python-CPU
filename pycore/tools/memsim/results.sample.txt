=== E6  dependent-chain length: line caching vs result caching ===
opcode              execs  acc/exec  now(3cyc)  perfectL1D  resultcache
LOAD_GLOBAL          3259      8.10       24.3         8.1          1.0
CALL                 3283      6.94       20.8         6.9          1.0
RETURN_VALUE         3243      4.00       12.0         4.0          1.0

STORE_NAME             27     13.41       40.2        13.4          1.0
LOAD_NAME              10      8.20       24.6         8.2          1.0

  A perfect L1D still walks every link of the chain at 1 cycle each.
  A result cache (const / global IC / code descriptor) collapses the whole
  chain into a single tag compare.

=== E8  line-aligning the heap allocator (16 B today -> 64 B) ===
align     lines     512B/2w    1024B/2w    2048B/2w    2048B/4w
16 B        151      75.96%      83.92%      99.31%      99.38%
64 B        141      84.54%      91.19%      99.39%      99.43%

  Saturates by 2 KB, so the payoff is a *smaller* L1D for the same
  hit rate: 64 B alignment at 1 KB beats 16 B alignment at 1 KB by
  7 points and cuts misses ~45%.
